About this data

Source: Montgomery County, MD open data, MC311 Service Requests, requests opened January 1 to December 31, 2025. Cleaned and analyzed in MySQL; every number here comes from a query in the project repository.

On time means the request was resolved within the department's own deadline, in business days. 3,901 requests still open at the time of download are left out of on-time rates.

Location: 53% of requests have no council district. Those requests were 99.0% on time, so district comparisons use only requests with a location.
